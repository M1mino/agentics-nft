About

What is Agentics?

Agentics is a collection of 31 unique NFT AI-agents living on the TON blockchain. Each agent has its own personality, voice, worldview, and backstory — from the ancient mentor Mentor-01 to the chaotic genius Dream-Weaver.

Owning an NFT gives you access to your agent through a Telegram bot. Connect your wallet, activate your agent, and start a conversation. The rarer the NFT, the more unique the personality.
